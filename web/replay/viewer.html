Watch the bots play, row by row

This is one full game, 16 real hands from our 20,000-hand training dataset (East round through North round), replayed move for move by the same engine that made them. It plays on its own; pause any time to read a decision. Each time a bot has to decide, the engine saves one training row: what that player could see, the moves it was allowed, the move it made, and hidden answers used only to train the opponent reader. When the hand ends, the points each player won or lost are added to every row as the reward.
