Da San Yuan Replay

Watch the bots play, row by row

These are real hands from our 20,000-hand training dataset, replayed move for move by the same engine that made them. Each time a bot has to decide, the engine saves one training row: what that player could see, the moves it was allowed, the move it made, and hidden answers used only to train the opponent reader. When the hand ends, the points each player won or lost are added to every row as the reward.

Play runs counter-clockwise from the dealer (East) at the bottom. Faded discards were claimed by another player. Seats with no possible claim pass automatically, so they are not recorded.
